Bar Chart · Histogram
Tidwell: Bar chart / histogram. Shows the distribution of books across genres in the BookList. Placed on the Statistics page to help users understand their reading diversity. Each bar uses the genre color from the style sheet.

🌳 Chart 4 — Book Category Dendrogram (Tree Diagram)

Dendrogram · Tree Diagram
Tidwell: Dendrogram / Tree diagram. Shows the hierarchical grouping of books in the BookList by category and subcategory. This tree structure mirrors the information architecture of the BookList app and helps users understand how books are organised. Placed on the Statistics / Info page.

📊 Chart 5 — Reading Activity by Month (Horizontal Bar)

Horizontal Bar Chart
Tidwell: Horizontal bar chart. Useful when category labels are long. Shows how many books were read each month. Placed in the Statistics page Reading Activity section.
